I am actively involved in research that focuses on advancing techniques for visual analytics and enhancing educational experiences. My work spans several key areas:

1. **Improving Visual Analytics Techniques** \\
I examine popular methods like t-SNE and UMAP, analyzing their limitations to foster more cautious and effective usage in high-dimensional data analysis. This research aims to improve the reliability and interpretability of visual analytics in complex data environments.


2. **Enhancing Lecture Review Experiences** \\
I am working on developing systems that enrich the review experience of live lectures. By capturing the dynamic and context-rich nature of live teaching, our goal is to help learners better retain and understand content, making learning more engaging and effective.


3. **Measuring Dataset Complexity for Better Dimensionality Reduction** \\
In order to make dimensionality reduction more efficient and reliable, I am involved in creating metrics that quantify the structural complexity of datasets. This work is focused on helping data scientists choose the most suitable reduction techniques, ultimately supporting more accurate and insightful data analysis.
