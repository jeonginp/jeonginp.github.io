### Overview

BugRider is a 3D racing adventure game where players ride insect mounts through fantastical environments.

**Ranked 1st in the popularity vote and 1st in the final evaluation among 12 teams.**

### My Role

- **Team Lead**: Managed 5 developers, ran planning and task allocation, and kept the schedule on track
- **Unity Developer**: Implemented game controls and audio systems
- **3D Asset Designer**: Modeled and animated all insect characters in **Blender**
