_A Unity-based 3D racing adventure game featuring hand-crafted insect characters_

### Overview

**Ranked 1st in popularity vote and 1st in final evaluation among 12 teams.**

Led a **5-member development team** to create BugRider, a 3D racing adventure game where players ride insect mounts through fantastical environments. Managed project planning, task allocation, and development schedule while contributing to both art and engineering.

### My Contributions

- **Team Lead**: Managed 5 developers, coordinated sprints, and maintained project timeline
- **Unity Developer**: Implemented game controls and audio systems
- **3D Asset Designer**: Modeled and animated insect characters using **Blender**:
